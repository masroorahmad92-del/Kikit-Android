# Kikit Child Safety Standards

*Last updated: October 2026*

Kikit (operated by KMZ ENTERTAINMENT LLC) is an adults-only dating and live video app. We have zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE), including child sexual abuse material (CSAM), grooming, sextortion, and any sexualisation of minors.

## 1. Adults only
- Kikit is only for people aged 18 and over. Users must give their date of birth at sign-up, and accounts under 18 are blocked.
- Every member must pass face verification, matching a live selfie to their profile photos, before they can join live rooms or message other members.
- We remove any account we believe belongs to a minor.

## 2. What is prohibited
Users may not create, upload, share, request, or link to:
- Any sexual or sexualised content involving a person under 18, real or computer-generated
- Any content or messages meant to groom, entice, or exploit a minor
- Any attempt to arrange contact with a minor, on or off Kikit

## 3. How we prevent and detect abuse
- Automated moderation of chat and live room messages
- Human review of reports and of flagged profiles, photos, and live rooms
- Blocking and reporting tools available on every profile, chat, and live room

## 4. How to report
- **In the app:** tap the ⋮ menu on any profile, message, or live room, then choose **Report**. You can also block the user.
- **By email:** admin@letskikit.com (subject: "Child Safety")
- If a child is in immediate danger, contact local emergency services first.

## 5. What we do when we find CSAE
- We remove the content and permanently ban the account.
- We preserve relevant evidence as the law requires.
- We report confirmed CSAM to the **National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC)** through the CyberTipline, and to other regional and national authorities where required.
- We cooperate with law enforcement requests.

## 6. Child safety point of contact
Our designated child safety contact can be reached at **admin@letskikit.com**. They are able to discuss our CSAM prevention practices and compliance.
